OUR STORY · 705 W. CROSS ST.

A Place to Call Your Own.

Elevated food, reasonable prices, and a warm welcome—that’s the founding idea behind Vinny’s. We’ve transformed the storied former Wurst Bar into a vibrant gathering spot for our Ypsilanti neighbors and the EMU community, pairing thoughtfully prepared favorites with carefully made cocktails.

Whether you're stopping in for a quick lunch between lectures on campus, unwinding after work with happy hour craft pints, or gathering friends to watch the Eagles play, Vinny’s is built around honest Midwestern hospitality.
