history_edu Local Heritage • Formerly the iconic Wurst Bar

Lovingly reimagined as Vinny's for the modern Cross Street community.

Elevated comfort food, fair prices, and a warm welcome every time you walk through the door. We transformed the storied former Wurst Bar on West Cross into a comfortable, vibrant home base for our Ypsilanti neighbors, EMU students, and collegiate alumni.
